Playing

How do I play?

Pop the bubbles that light up — fast and accurately. Choose a mode on the main screen and tap PLAY. Tapping a bubble that isn't lit costs you, so precision matters as much as speed.

What are the game modes?

  • Score — pop as many lit bubbles as you can before the timer runs out. Quick hits build a combo multiplier; misses break it.
  • Reflex — wait for a bubble to flash and tap it as fast as possible. Your reaction time is measured in milliseconds. Tapping too early counts as a false start.
  • Memory — watch the sequence of bubbles, then repeat it. Each round adds one more step.
  • Stages — 30 hand-made levels with their own goals. Earn up to 3 stars per level.
  • Pass & Play — 2 to 4 players take turns on one device. Names are optional.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Every day there is a new challenge, the same for every player. Your first attempt is the official one; after that you can keep practising. Play every day to build a streak.

What are seasonal events?

From time to time a limited-time event appears, with its own challenges and a free event theme while it runs. A gift icon on the main screen shows when an event is live.

Can I pause a game?

Yes. Tap the pause button during play, or simply leave the app — the game pauses automatically. Tap RESUME to continue after a short countdown.

Progress

Where can I see my records and achievements?

Open Settings (the gear icon at the top right) and tap Records.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically after every game. Your progress is stored only on your device, so it is not linked to an account.

I deleted the app or got a new phone. Is my progress gone?

Unfortunately, records, stars and achievements are stored only on the device and are removed when you delete the app. A new iPhone restored from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one normally brings the game data along. Premium is never lost — see Restore Purchases.

How do I change the look of the game?

Go to Settings ▸ Style to choose a theme, bubble style and sound pack.

Premium & purchases

What do I get with Premium?

Pop Pulse Premium is a single, one-time purchase — no subscription. It unlocks:

  • all 30 Stage levels (the first 12 are free);
  • full Reflex stats and graph;
  • 5 premium themes;
  • Gem and Pearl bubbles;
  • Cosmic and Deep Sea sound packs;
  • no ads, ever.

Find it under Settings ▸ Premium. The price is shown in your local currency.

I bought Premium but it isn't unlocked

Go to Settings ▸ Purchases ▸ RESTORE PURCHASES while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Premium is also restored automatically when you reinstall the app or use a new device, and it works for family members through Family Sharing.

My purchase says "Waiting for approval"

This happens with Ask to Buy in Family Sharing. Once the family organiser approves the request, Premium unlocks automatically — no need to buy again.

How do I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads & privacy

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep Pop Pulse free. They never appear during play. Occasionally a full-screen ad appears between games, and some options — such as continuing a run, one more official Daily Challenge try, or testing a premium theme — let you choose to watch a short video. Premium removes all ads.

Why does the game ask about tracking?

After your first game, Pop Pulse explains how ads work and iOS asks whether ads may use your activity to be more relevant. Where required, you are also asked for ad consent first. Either choice is fine — the game plays exactly the same; you just see less relevant ads if you decline.

How do I change my ad or tracking choice?

In the game: Settings ▸ Privacy & Legal ▸ CHANGE AD CONSENT. This button appears in regions where ad consent is required by law (such as the EU, UK and Switzerland), after your first game. For tracking: iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.

What data do you collect?

No account is needed and we don't collect your name or email. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Sound & accessibility

How do I turn sound or vibration off?

Open Settings and use the Sound and Haptics switches. Your choice is remembered. Pop Pulse also respects your iPhone's silent switch.

The animations are too much for me

Under Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Reduce motion choose On for calm fades instead of bouncing and flashing effects. Like iOS follows your iPhone's own Reduce Motion setting.

I find it hard to tell the colours apart

Turn on Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Symbols on lit bubbles so lit bubbles are marked with a symbol as well as colour.

Problems

The game feels slow or stutters

Close other apps, make sure Low Power Mode is off, and restart the game. If it keeps happening, email us with your device model and iOS version.

My taps don't seem to register

Remove any screen protector smudges and make sure you tap the lit bubble itself. In Reflex, wait for the flash — tapping too early is counted as a false start.

I found a bug or the app crashed

Please email digitaldiamonds.ai@gmail.com with what happened, the game mode, your device model, iOS version and app version. Screenshots or a screen recording help a lot.
