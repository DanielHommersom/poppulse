Pop Pulse

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress, records, achievements and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it we ask for your consent first, and iOS asks whether ads may track you. Saying no is always fine — the game plays exactly the same.
  • With Premium you never see ads, and the advertising SDK is never started.
  • We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Pop Pulse ("the App") is a game developed and published by Digital Diamonds, based in the Netherlands ("we", "us"). We are the controller for the limited processing described below. You can reach us at digitaldiamonds.ai@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not run our own user accounts or servers that collect data about you. The App does not ask for, and we do not receive, your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal identifiers. The only data handled outside your device is described in the sections below: advertising (free version), purchases, and a small events file the App downloads.

Game data stored on your device

Your progress (Stage stars, best scores, Reflex times, Daily Challenge results and streaks), unlocked achievements, chosen theme, bubble style and sound pack, and your settings (sound, haptics, reduce motion and other accessibility options) are saved in a file on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the App. Pass & Play player names, if you enter any, are also kept only on your device.

Advertising (free version)

The free version of Pop Pulse shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC): optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example to continue a run, get one more official Daily Challenge try, play a Premium level once, or try a premium theme, bubble style or sound pack), and occasional full-screen ads between games. Ads are never shown during play.

To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process:

  • the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS) — only if you allow tracking;
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, completed videos).

Consent

The first time ads could be shown (after your first game, never at launch), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it — for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and certain US states. After that, Pop Pulse shows a short explanation and then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Ads are personalised only if you give consent and allow tracking; otherwise you receive non-personalised ads, which do not use the advertising identifier and are based on limited data such as the context and coarse location.

You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings ▸ Privacy & Legal ▸ Change ad consent (shown in regions where consent is required), and in iOS under Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases (Premium)

Pop Pulse Premium is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. To confirm and restore your purchase we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives the App Store transaction record, a random anonymous app user ID generated by the App, and basic device and app information. This lets Premium be restored automatically after reinstalling, on a new device or through Family Sharing. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Seasonal events

To show seasonal events, the App downloads a small events file from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). No personal data is sent with this request. Like any website, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes; we do not receive or use it to identify you. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing and links

When you use Tell a friend, the App opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message; you decide whether and to whom to send it. Sharing a result opens the iOS share sheet. Links to this policy and the Terms of Use open in your browser. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own.
  • No Game Center, leaderboards, social login, chat or multiplayer over the internet. Pass & Play happens on one device.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

  • Storing your progress and providing Premium: performance of our agreement with you.
  • Personalised ads and access to the advertising identifier: your consent.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and server logs: our legitimate interest in keeping the App free and secure.

International transfers

Google, RevenueCat and GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries. They rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention

Data on your device remains until you delete the App. Google and RevenueCat keep data according to their own retention policies. Hosting logs for the events file are kept by GitHub according to its own policy.

Children

Pop Pulse is a general-audience game rated 12+ on the App Store and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Because we do not link data to your identity, most of these rights are exercised directly: delete the App to remove local data, use Change ad consent or the iOS Tracking setting for ads, and contact Google or RevenueCat for data they hold. You can always email us for help, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority — in the Netherlands the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
