**Description**
```
Pop the glowing bubbles before they fade.

Pop Pulse is a fast, satisfying neon arcade game for quick sessions. Bubbles light up on the grid — tap them in time, build your combo and chase a new personal best. Every pop clicks, glows and buzzes under your finger.

FIVE WAYS TO PLAY
• Score — Pop as many lit bubbles as you can in 60 seconds. Quick hits build a combo multiplier of up to ×8; misses break it.
• Reflex — Wait for the flash and tap. Your reaction time in milliseconds, with no luck involved.
• Memory — Watch the pattern, then repeat it. One more step every round.
• Stages — 30 handcrafted levels with their own goals. Earn up to three stars on each.
• Pass & Play — 2 to 4 players take turns on one phone. Perfect for the couch, the car or the classroom.

A NEW CHALLENGE EVERY DAY
The Daily Challenge is the same for every player. Your first try is the official one — keep your streak alive and practise as often as you like.

SEASONAL EVENTS
Limited-time events with their own challenges and exclusive themes to keep.

MAKE IT YOURS
Unlock themes, bubble styles and sound packs, and collect achievements along the way.

PLAYS THE WAY YOU DO
• No account, no sign-in, works offline
• Short rounds — one game takes about a minute
• Reduce Motion and symbol options for comfortable play
• Free to play with optional ads that never appear during a game
```
